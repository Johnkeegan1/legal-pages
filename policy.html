SMS Privacy Policy

This SMS Privacy Policy explains how Ocean Wise Conservation Association collects, uses, and protects personal information provided through its text messaging programs.

Scope

This policy applies only to information collected through Ocean Wise Conservation Association’s SMS text messaging services (“Text Message Service”). It does not apply to information collected through other websites, applications, or services.

Information We Collect

When you opt in to receive text messages, we may collect the following information:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Opt‑in and opt‑out consent records
  • Message interaction data (such as HELP or STOP requests)

How We Use Information

We use SMS‑related information solely to:

  • Send informational text messages
  • Provide Microsoft Teams alerts, notifications, and IT service updates
  • Respond to support requests
  • Maintain compliance and audit records

Data Sharing

Mobile opt‑in data, consent records, and phone numbers collected for SMS purposes will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share limited information with service providers (such as SMS platforms and mobile carriers) solely to deliver and operate the Text Message Service, or when required by law.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your interaction with the Text Message Service. Messages are informational in nature.

Your Choices

You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP. For help or support, reply HELP.

Data Security

Ocean Wise Conservation Association uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect SMS‑related information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this SMS Privacy Policy, contact us at IT@ocean.org.
