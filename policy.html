SMS Privacy Policy

This SMS Privacy Policy describes how Ocean Wise Conservation Association collects, uses, and protects personal information provided through its text message communications.

Scope

This policy applies only to information collected through our SMS text messaging programs (“Text Message Service”). It does not apply to other data collected through our website, applications, or services, which are governed by separate privacy notices.

Information We Collect

When you opt in to receive text messages from us, we may collect:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Opt‑in and opt‑out consent records
  • Message interaction data (such as HELP or STOP requests)

How We Use Your Information

We use SMS‑related information solely to:

  • Deliver informational text messages
  • Respond to inquiries or support requests
  • Maintain compliance records
  • Operate and improve our messaging services

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Mobile opt‑in data, consent information, and phone numbers collected for SMS purposes will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share necessary information with service providers that support message delivery (such as messaging platforms and carriers) solely to operate the Text Message Service, or when required by law.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your interaction with the Text Message Service. Messages are informational and may include Microsoft Teams alerts, notifications, and IT service updates.

Your Choices

You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request help by replying HELP. These commands are available at any time.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect SMS‑related information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this SMS Privacy Policy or our text messaging practices, please contact us at IT@ocean.org.
